Emberfall launcher art
Textures: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures (branch "live"), Blizzard Entertainment UI textures (PNG, no BLP conversion needed).
Processing: alpha or brightness crop; backdrop edge strips rebuilt as 3x3 nine-slice sheets (top/bottom segments rotated).
No logos are included.
